# P90X Kenpo X Workout Requirements

This document outlines the exercises, groupings, sequence, duration, and repetitions of the **P90X Kenpo X** workout parsed from the session transcript.

## General Structure

- **Warm-Up & Stretch**: ~11 minutes (dynamic and static flexibility)
- **Punch Sections**: Performed in rep-based sequences.
- **Cardio Breaks**: Short active recovery sequences (~2-3 minutes each) between major segments.
- **Kick Sections**: Rep-based kicking drills with target elevations.
- **Blocks & Elbows**: Defense and strike sequences.
- **Finishing Combos & Burnout**: High-intensity punch sequences.
- **Cool Down & Stretch**: ~5 minutes.
