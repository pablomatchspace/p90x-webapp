# P90X Cardio X Workout Requirements

This document outlines the exercises, groupings, sequence, and durations/repetitions of the **P90X Cardio X** workout parsed from the session transcript.

## General Structure
- **Warm-Up**: ~4 minutes (running, butt kicks, jacks, static hamstring/quad stretches).
- **Yoga Warm-Up**: Ashtanga Sun Salutations and Warrior poses linked by Vinyasa transitions.
- **Kenpo Karate**: Rep-based kicking and punching combinations.
- **Plyometrics**: 2 rounds of jump training (active intervals of 30 seconds).
- **Core Section**: Squat-jumps, twists, core rolls, and static/dynamic holds.
- **Cool Down & Stretch**: ~5 minutes.

---

## Exercise Directory by Sequence

### 1. Warm-Up Phase
Performed once sequentially.
- **Run in Place**: 60s
- **Butt Kicks (Knees down, heels up)**: 30s
- **Jump Rope (Low impact / kick out)**: 30s
- **Jumping Jacks**: 30s
- **Standing Wide-Leg Forward Fold (Rocking)**: 30s
- **Pyramid Hamstring Stretch (Right leg forward)**: 30s (drive left hip to right ankle)
- **Pyramid Hamstring Stretch (Left leg forward)**: 30s (drive right hip to left ankle)
- **Standing Quad Stretch**: 60s (30s right side, 30s left side)

---

### 2. Yoga Warm-Up Series
Continuous flow. Stances hold for ~30 seconds.
- **Sun Salutation Vinyasa 1**: Arms Up -> Swan Dive -> Flat Back -> Plank -> Chaturanga -> Upward Dog -> Plank -> Push-up -> Downward Dog (hold 30s) -> Jump to hands -> Flat Back -> Fold -> Reverse Swan Dive.
- **Sun Salutation Vinyasa 2**: Same sequence (Downward Dog hold 30s).
- **Runner's Lunge (Right)**: Vinyasa entry to Downward Dog -> Float right heel high -> Swing through to Runner's Pose (30s) -> Vinyasa exit.
- **Runner's Lunge (Left)**: Downward Dog -> Float left heel high -> Swing through to Runner's Pose (30s) -> Vinyasa exit.
- **Warrior One (Right)**: Vinyasa -> Float right heel -> Swing through -> Warrior One (back heel down, 30s) -> Vinyasa exit.
- **Warrior One (Left)**: Downward Dog -> Float left heel -> Swing through -> Warrior One (30s) -> Vinyasa exit.
- **Warrior Two (Right)**: Vinyasa -> Float right heel -> Swing through -> Warrior One -> Warrior Two (30s) -> Vinyasa exit.
- **Warrior Two (Left)**: Downward Dog -> Float left heel -> Swing through -> Warrior One -> Warrior Two (30s) -> Vinyasa exit.
- **Reverse Warrior (Right)**: Vinyasa -> Float right heel -> Swing through -> Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior (30s) -> Vinyasa exit.
- **Reverse Warrior (Left)**: Downward Dog -> Float left heel -> Swing through -> Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior (30s) -> Vinyasa exit -> Downward Dog -> Jump/walk feet to hands.

---

### 3. Kenpo Karate Series
Rep-based martial arts drills. Hands up, guard ribs.
- **Ball Kicks / Front Kicks (Right leg / Left stance)**: 20 reps
- **Ball Kicks / Front Kicks (Left leg / Right stance)**: 20 reps
- **Hook, Uppercut, Low Sidekick (Left stance)**: 15 reps
- **Hook, Uppercut, Low Sidekick (Right stance)**: 15 reps
- **Back Knuckles, Ball Kick, Back Kick (Left stance)**: 15 reps (look back, front, back)
- **Back Knuckles, Ball Kick, Back Kick (Right stance)**: 15 reps
- **Jab, Cross, Hook, Uppercut (Left stance)**: 25 reps (hip-driven rotation)
- **Jab, Cross, Hook, Uppercut (Right stance)**: 25 reps
- **Three Direction Kicks (Front, Side, Back - Left kick/Right support)**: 6 full combinations (18 total kicks)
- **Three Direction Kicks (Front, Side, Back - Right kick/Left support)**: 6 full combinations (18 total kicks)

---

### 4. Plyometrics Series (Rounds 1 & 2)
Perform the sequence below twice. Stances are timed intervals of 30 seconds.
- **Airborne Heisman**: 30s (twice total - shuffle side-to-side with balance pause)
- **Swing Kicks**: 30s (twice total - alternating legs over chair/stool)
- **Jump Shot**: 30s (twice total - Round 1: catch right/shoot left; Round 2: catch left/shoot right)
- **Monster Truck Tires**: 30s (twice total - 4 steps forward, 4 steps back, high knees)
- **Wacky Jacks**: 30s (twice total - lateral jacks crunched at waist)

---

### 5. Core Section
A combination of rep and timed core/abdominal builders.
- **Squat, Cross, X Press**: 30 reps (touch floor, reach wide; last 10 reps are jump-squats)
- **Steam Engine**: 50 reps (hands behind head, high knee twist; starts slow, picks up speed)
- **Dreya Roll**: 60s (roll back on spine, roll forward to squat and stand/jump)
- **Squat Run**: 60s total (30s left foot forward, 30s right foot forward; low run stance)
- **Superman / Banana**: 60s total (alternating holds on stomach [Superman] and back [Banana] on cue)

---

### 6. Cool Down & Stretch
Performed once sequentially.
- **Arm Shakers**: 30s
- **Jump Rope (Low Impact)**: 30s
- **Marching Kick**: 30s
- **Wide Forward Fold (Rocking)**: 60s
- **Downward Dog (Alternating Calf Stretch)**: 60s
- **Cat/Cow Stretch**: 15s (1 breathing cycle)
- **Standing Quad Stretch**: 60s (30s right side, 30s left side)
