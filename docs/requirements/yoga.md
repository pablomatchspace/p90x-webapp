# Yoga Requirements — two switchable variants

> App requirement: Yoga is **able to switch between P90X classic or P90X3 mode**. Both source documents below, verbatim.

# P90X Yoga X Workout Requirements

This document outlines the exercises, groupings, sequence, and durations of the **P90X Yoga X** workout parsed from the session transcript (up to the transcript cutoff point).

## General Structure
- **Warm-Up & Stretch**: ~7 minutes of static and dynamic prep stretches.
- **Ashtanga Sun Salutations (Vinyasas)**: 3 continuous rounds.
- **Vinyasa Flow - Runner's Series**: High/low lunges integrated with Vinyasa transitions.
- **Standing Pose Flow (Warrior Series)**: Progression of Warrior poses, Triangle poses, and Balance holds on each side, linked by Vinyasas.
- **Chair Pose Series**: Quad and core holds with balance and twists.
- **Vinyasa Flow - Knee-to-Forehead & Bound Angles**: Core knee tucks and deep bound stretches.

---

## Exercise Directory by Sequence

### 1. Warm-Up & Prep Stretches
Performed once sequentially.
- **Mountain Pose**: ~30s (5 deep breaths for mental focus)
- **Wide Forward Fold (Swan Dive)**: ~1-2 min (arms folded, hanging stretch)
- **Wide Leg Twist (Right arm up)**: 30s (5 breaths, left hand down/on block)
- **Wide Leg Twist (Left arm up)**: 30s (5 breaths, right hand down/on block)
- **Pyramid Hamstring Stretch (Right leg forward)**: 60s (hips squared, hands down)
- **Pyramid Hamstring Stretch (Left leg forward)**: 60s
- **Standing Side Stretch (Left side reach)**: 30s (right hip kicked out, left hand down, right arm over)
- **Standing Side Stretch (Right side reach)**: 30s (left hip kicked out, right hand down, left arm over)

---

### 2. Ashtanga Sun Salutations (Vinyasa Cycles)
Continuous flow. Perform 3 complete rounds.
- **Vinyasa Cycle 1**:
  - Inhale Arms Up -> Exhale Swan Dive -> Flat Back Inhale -> Step/Jump to Plank -> Chaturanga -> Upward Dog -> Downward Dog (hold ~30s) -> Jump to Hands -> Flat Back -> Forward Fold -> Reverse Swan Dive.
- **Vinyasa Cycle 2**:
  - Inhale Arms Up -> Exhale Swan Dive -> Flat Back Inhale -> Step/Jump to Plank -> Chaturanga -> Upward Dog -> Downward Dog (hold ~30s) -> Jump to Hands -> Flat Back -> Forward Fold -> Reverse Swan Dive.
- **Vinyasa Cycle 3**:
  - Inhale Arms Up -> Exhale Swan Dive -> Flat Back Inhale -> Step/Jump to Plank -> Chaturanga -> Upward Dog -> Downward Dog (hold ~30s) -> Jump to Hands -> Flat Back -> Forward Fold -> Reverse Swan Dive.

---

### 3. Vinyasa Flow - Runner's Series
Vinyasa entry, runner splits, and exits.
- **Runner's Pose (Right leg forward)**:
  - Vinyasa entry to Downward Dog -> Float right heel high -> Swing through to low lunge (fingertips touching) -> Lower to elbows (deep groin stretch): ~30s
  - Exit: Back to Plank -> Vinyasa -> Downward Dog
- **Runner's Pose (Left leg forward)**:
  - Float left heel high -> Swing through to low lunge -> Lower to elbows: ~30s
  - Exit: Back to Plank -> Vinyasa -> Downward Dog

---

### 4. Standing Pose Flow (Warrior Series)
Linked sequentially. Each stance holds for ~30-45 seconds.
- **Crescent Pose (Right leg)**: Vinyasa entry -> Right leg swing through -> Crescent Pose -> Vinyasa exit
- **Crescent Pose (Left leg)**: Left leg swing through -> Crescent Pose -> Vinyasa exit
- **Warrior One (Right leg)**: Right leg swing through -> Warrior One (back heel down) -> Vinyasa exit
- **Warrior One (Left leg)**: Left leg swing through -> Warrior One -> Vinyasa exit
- **Warrior Two (Right leg)**: Right leg swing through -> Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Vinyasa exit
- **Warrior Two (Left leg)**: Left leg swing through -> Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Vinyasa exit
- **Reverse Warrior (Right leg)**: Right leg swing through -> Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior -> Vinyasa exit
- **Reverse Warrior (Left leg)**: Left leg swing through -> Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior -> Vinyasa exit
- **Triangle Pose (Right leg)**: Right leg swing through -> Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior (straighten leg) -> Warrior Two -> Triangle Pose (reach left hip back, right hand to floor/block/leg, left arm up) -> Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior -> Warrior Two -> Warrior One -> Vinyasa exit
- **Triangle Pose (Left leg)**: Left leg swing through -> Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior (straighten leg) -> Warrior Two -> Triangle Pose -> Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior -> Warrior Two -> Warrior One -> Vinyasa exit
- **Twisting Triangle (Right leg)**: Right leg swing through -> Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Straighten leg -> Twisting Triangle (left hand to floor/block, right arm up) -> Vinyasa exit
- **Twisting Triangle (Left leg)**: Left leg swing through -> Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Straighten leg -> Twisting Triangle (right hand to floor/block, left arm up) -> Vinyasa exit

---

### 5. Chair Pose Series
High-intensity static holds.
- **Chair Pose (Basic)**: ~30s (arms straight, hips dropped, feet/knees together)
- **Tiptoe Balance Hold**: ~15s (rise onto tiptoes in Chair Pose)
- **Twisting Chair Pose (Left twist)**: ~30s (left elbow outside right knee, hands in prayer)
- **Tiptoe Balance Hold**: ~15s
- **Twisting Chair Pose (Right twist)**: ~30s (right elbow outside left knee, hands in prayer)
- **Tiptoe Balance Hold**: ~15s
- *Optional Brief Water Break*

---

### 6. Vinyasa Flow - Knee-to-Forehead & Bound Angles
Advanced core integration and binding holds.
- **Knee-to-Forehead Core Flow (Right leg)**:
  - Vinyasa entry to Downward Dog -> Float right heel high -> Knee-to-forehead tuck (3 times) -> Swing through to Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior -> Warrior Two.
- **Right Angle Pose (Right leg)**: ~30s (right hand inside right foot, left arm up)
- **Extended Right Angle Pose (Right leg)**: ~30s (sweep left arm overhead, forming straight line from heel to fingers)
- **Bound Right Angle / Half-Bind (Right leg)**: ~30s (right arm under thigh, left hand behind back, clasp hands/wrist)
- **Exit Sequence**: Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior -> Warrior Two -> Warrior One -> Vinyasa exit
- **Knee-to-Forehead Core Flow (Left leg)**:
  - Float left heel high -> Knee-to-forehead tuck (3 times) -> Swing through to Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior -> Warrior Two.
- **Right Angle Pose (Left leg)**: ~30s
- **Extended Right Angle Pose (Left leg)**: ~30s
- **Bound Right Angle / Half-Bind (Left leg)**: ~30s
- **Exit Sequence**: Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior -> Warrior Two -> Warrior One -> Vinyasa exit
- **Prayer Twist in Lunge (Right leg)**:
  - Float right heel high -> Swing through to Runner's Pose -> Prayer Twist (left elbow outside right knee, palms pressed) [Transcript Cutoff]

# P90X3 Yoga Workout Requirements

This document outlines the exercises, groupings, sequence, and durations of the **P90X3 Yoga** (30-Minute) workout parsed from the session transcript.

## General Structure
- **Warm-Up**: ~2 minutes (Child's Pose, Downward Dog, Forward Fold).
- **Sun Salutation A**: 3 rounds.
- **Crescent Series**: Crescent Lunge, Airplane, and Shoulder Stretch.
- **Sun Salutation B (Warrior & Wraps)**: Warrior 1, Warrior 2, Reverse Warrior, and Bound Side Angle.
- **Balance & Splits**: Warrior 3, Half Moon, Revolved Half Moon, and Standing Splits.
- **Wide Angle & Triangles**: Straddle Folds, Triangles, and Twisting Triangles.
- **Standing Balances & Crow**: Tree, Toe Binds, Figure 4 balances, and Crow Pose.
- **Spine & Floor Series**: Bird Dog, Camel, Figure 4 Hamstring, Shoulder Stand, Plow, Fish, and Shavasana.

---

## Exercise Directory by Sequence

### 1. Warm-Up Phase
Performed once sequentially.
- **Child's Pose**: 45s
- **Downward Dog**: 30s
- **Forward Fold (Forward Hang)**: 30s
- **Standing Roll Up**: 15s

---

### 2. Sun Salutation A
Continuous flow. Perform 3 complete rounds.
- **Round 1**: Arms Up -> Swan Dive -> Flat Back -> Plank -> Chaturanga -> Upward Dog -> Downward Dog (hold 15s) -> Step/Jump to Hands -> Halfway Up -> Forward Fold -> Reverse Swan Dive.
- **Round 2**: Arms Up -> Swan Dive -> Flat Back -> Float/Step to Plank -> Chaturanga -> Upward Dog -> Downward Dog (hold 15s) -> Float/Jump to Hands -> Halfway Up -> Forward Fold -> Reverse Swan Dive.
- **Round 3**: Arms Up -> Swan Dive -> Flat Back -> Float/Step to Plank -> Chaturanga -> Upward Dog -> Push-up -> Downward Dog (with 6 alternating calf pedals) -> Float/Jump to Hands -> Halfway Up -> Forward Fold -> Reverse Swan Dive.

---

### 3. Crescent Series
Linked by Vinyasas. Stances hold for ~30 seconds.
- **Crescent Lunge (Right side)**: Vinyasa entry -> Right leg swing through -> Crescent Lunge -> Airplane Pose (arms wide) -> Airplane with Clasp (clasp hands behind back, drop chest inside knee) -> Crescent Lunge -> Vinyasa exit.
- **Crescent Lunge (Left side)**: Left leg swing through -> Crescent Lunge -> Airplane Pose -> Airplane with Clasp -> Crescent Lunge -> Vinyasa exit.

---

### 4. Sun Salutation B & Bound Side Angle Series
- **Vinyasa Cycle to Warrior 1 (Right side)**: Warrior One (back heel down, 15s) -> Warrior Two (15s) -> Reverse Warrior (15s) -> Bound Side Angle / Forearm on Thigh (30s) -> Warrior Two -> Warrior One -> Vinyasa exit.
- **Vinyasa Cycle to Warrior 1 (Left side)**: Warrior One -> Warrior Two -> Reverse Warrior -> Bound Side Angle / Forearm on Thigh -> Warrior Two -> Warrior One -> Vinyasa exit.
- **Chair Pose**: Jump/step to hands -> Chair Pose (knees/feet together, weight in heels): 30s
- **Exit Sequence**: Stand up -> Backbend -> Swan Dive -> Vinyasa exit.

---

### 5. Balance & Split Series
Linked by Crescent Lunge transitions and Vinyasa exits.
- **Warrior Balance (Right side support)**:
  - Crescent Lunge -> Warrior Three (raise left leg, 30s) -> Half Moon Pose (right hand down, left arm/hip open, 30s) -> Revolved Half Moon (left hand down, right arm/hip open, 30s) -> Standing Square Splits (both hands down, nose to knee, left leg up, 30s) -> Crescent Lunge -> Vinyasa exit.
- **Warrior Balance (Left side support)**:
  - Crescent Lunge -> Warrior Three (raise right leg, 30s) -> Half Moon Pose (left hand down, right arm/hip open, 30s) -> Revolved Half Moon (right hand down, left arm/hip open, 30s) -> Standing Square Splits (both hands down, nose to knee, right leg up, 30s) -> Crescent Lunge -> Vinyasa exit.
- **Chair Pose**: Jump to hands -> Chair Pose: 30s
- **Exit**: Stand up -> Prayer.

---

### 6. Wide Stance & Triangle Series
Stretches performed sequentially.
- **Wide-Leg Forward Fold**: 45s (straddle mat, toes/heels aligned, fold forward, grab toes) -> Flat Back inhale -> Stand up.
- **Triangle Pose (Right side)**: 30s (turn right toe out, reach right arm down, left arm up)
- **Triangle Pose (Left side)**: 30s (turn left toe out, reach left arm down, right arm up)
- **Twisting Triangle (Right side)**: 30s (left hand inside/outside right foot or on block, right arm up)
- **Twisting Triangle (Left side)**: 30s (right hand inside/outside left foot, left arm up)

---

### 7. Standing Balances & Crow Pose
Balance checks.
- **Tree Pose (Right side support)**: 30s (left foot on inner right thigh, hands in prayer or overhead)
- **Tree Pose (Left side support)**: 30s (right foot on inner left thigh)
- **Extended Leg with Toe Bind (Right side support)**: 30s (grab left big toe, extend left leg forward/out)
- **Extended Leg with Toe Bind (Left side support)**: 30s (grab right big toe, extend right leg forward/out)
- **Ted's Chair / Figure 4 Balance (Right side support)**: 30s (cross left ankle above right knee, drop hips into single-leg chair; optional arm balance)
- **Ted's Chair / Figure 4 Balance (Left side support)**: 30s (cross right ankle above left knee, drop hips; optional arm balance)
- **Crow Pose**: 30s (knees on triceps, float feet; 3 breaths) -> Chaturanga shootback -> Vinyasa -> Child's Pose (brief).

---

### 8. Spine & Floor Series
Floor work.
- **Cat/Dog Tilt (Cat/Cow)**: 45s (3 breathing cycles on hands and knees)
- **Bird Dog (Right side / Dog Dancer)**: 30s (right arm forward, left leg back; grab ankle to lift chest)
- **Bird Dog (Left side / Dog Dancer)**: 30s (left arm forward, right leg back; grab ankle)
- **Camel Pose**: 30s (knees down, hips forward, hands to heels, head back; 5 breaths)
- **Figure 4 Hamstring Series (Right leg straight)**:
  - Seated Single-Leg Fold: 30s (left foot to inner thigh, fold forward)
  - Seated Figure Four Fold: 30s (left ankle on right thigh, fold forward)
  - Seated Figure Four Ankle-to-Butt Pull: 30s (pigeon/glute pull)
  - Seated Twist: 30s (drop left foot to floor outside right leg, twist right)
- **Figure 4 Hamstring Series (Left leg straight)**:
  - Seated Single-Leg Fold: 30s
  - Seated Figure Four Fold: 30s
  - Seated Figure Four Ankle-to-Butt Pull: 30s
  - Seated Twist: 30s
- **Plow Pose**: 30s (lie on back, drop feet overhead)
- **Shoulder Stand**: 30s (support lower back, legs vertical)
- **Vertebrae Roll Down**: 15s (slow roll out to knees-to-ears)
- **Fish Pose**: 30s (up on forearms, chest up, head back)
- **Shavasana (Final Relaxation)**: 1-2 min (lying flat)
