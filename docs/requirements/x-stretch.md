# P90X X Stretch Workout Requirements

This document outlines the exercises, groupings, sequence, and durations of the **P90X X Stretch** workout parsed from the session transcript.

## General Structure
- **Warm-Up / Sun Salutations**: 3 rounds.
- **Joint & Spine Mobilization**: Neck, wrist, forearm, and spinal stretches.
- **Upper Body Stretches**: Shoulders, chest, triceps, and upper back.
- **Core & Spine Stretches**: Back rolling, shoulder stand, plow, and seated twists.
- **Lower Body Stretches**: Deep glute, hamstring, quad, inner thigh, and calf stretches.
- **Ankle & Shin Mobility**: Point-flex and rotation drills.
- **Cool Down**: Child's pose variations.

---

## Exercise Directory by Sequence

### 1. Warm-Up (Sun Salutations)
Continuous flow. Perform 3 complete rounds.
- **Sun Salutation Round 1**: Arms up -> Swan dive -> Flat back -> Plank -> Chaturanga -> Upward Dog -> Downward Dog (hold 30s) -> Jump to hands -> Flat back -> Forward Fold -> Reverse Swan Dive.
- **Sun Salutation Round 2**: Arms up -> Swan dive -> Flat back -> Plank -> Chaturanga -> Upward Dog -> Push-up -> Downward Dog (hold 15s) -> Jump to hands -> Flat back -> Forward Fold -> Reverse Swan Dive.
- **Sun Salutation Round 3**: Arms up -> Swan dive -> Flat back -> Plank -> Chaturanga -> Upward Dog -> Push-up -> Downward Dog (hold 15s) -> Jump to hands -> Flat back -> Forward Fold -> Reverse Swan Dive (tiptoe reach at top).

---

### 2. Neck & Spine Mobilization
Performed once sequentially.
- **Neck Stretch (Right wrist pull)**: ~45s (ear to left shoulder; look up, look down, look forward)
- **Neck Stretch (Left wrist pull)**: ~45s (ear to right shoulder; look up, look down, look forward)
- **Spinal Twist (Backing up the car)**: 15s Left / 15s Right (wide feet, torso rotation)
- **Head Rolls**: 60s total (6 cycles, active hands pointing down)
- **Expand-Contract Chest Stretch**: 30s (deep horse stance, pull arms wide)
- **Tapas Stretch**: 45s (3 cycles; horse stance, thumbs back, exhale forward)

---

### 3. Wrist & Forearm Stretches
Performed once sequentially on hands and knees (tabletop).
- **Side-to-Side Wrist Rocks**: 30s (10 rock cycles left/right)
- **Fingertips Back Flexor Hold**: 30s (fingers point to knees, palms flat, shift weight back)
- **Dreya Forearm Stretch**: 60s (2 cycles; self-created tension pulling hands forward/back)

---

### 4. Upper Body Stretches
Performed once sequentially.
- **Shoulder Circles**: 80s total (20s forward medium, 20s backward medium, 20s backward big, 20s forward big)
- **Cross-Body Arm Pull (Right)**: ~30s
- **Overhead Triceps Pull (Right)**: ~30s
- **Cross-Body Arm Pull (Left)**: ~30s
- **Overhead Triceps Pull (Left)**: ~30s
- **Ballistic Huggers (Chest Openers)**: 30s (alternating arm cross)
- **Wave Reachers**: 30s (hands up, fold forward, sweep arms back)
- **Swimmers (Backstroke/Front crawl)**: 30s (4 backstroke + 4 front crawl per arm)
- **Standing Side Stretch (Left side bend)**: 30s (left hand down, right arm over, right hip out)
- **Standing Side Stretch (Right side bend)**: 30s (right hand down, left arm over, left hip out)

---

### 5. Seated & Lying Core/Spine Stretches
Performed once sequentially.
- **Spine Rollers**: 30s (10 rolls forward/back hugging knees to chest)
- **Shoulder Stand**: 30s (vertical leg balance, supporting lower back)
- **Plow Pose**: 30s (feet drop overhead, hands clasped on floor)
- **Knees to Ears**: 15s (knees bent next to ears from plow)
- **Vertebrae Roll Down**: 15s (slow roll out to back)
- **Seated Spinal Twist (Right leg bent over)**: 45s (pretzel leg, twist right; 5 deep breaths)
- **Seated Spinal Twist (Left leg bent over)**: 45s (pretzel leg, twist left; 5 deep breaths)
- **Cat/Cow Stretch**: 60s (5 breathing cycles on hands and knees)

---

### 6. Seated Lower Body Stretches
Performed once sequentially.
- **Seated Glute Stretch (Right)**: 45s (right ankle on left knee, pull left thigh/shin)
- **Seated Glute Stretch (Left)**: 45s (left ankle on right knee, pull right thigh/shin)
- **Standing Wide-Leg Forward Fold**: 60s (feet wide, parallel, folding arms)
- **Wide Fold with Twist (Right arm up)**: 30s (left hand down, right arm up)
- **Wide Fold with Twist (Left arm up)**: 30s (right hand down, left arm up)
- **Camel Pose**: 45s (knees on floor, hips forward, arch back, hands to heels)
- **Cat/Cow Counter**: 15s (1 breathing cycle)
- **Hero Pose**: 60s (sitting between heels; hands, elbows, or flat on back)
- **Kenpo Quad Stretch (Right knee forward)**: 30s (pigeon stance, bend left knee, grab left foot)
- **Kenpo Quad Stretch (Left knee forward)**: 30s (pigeon stance, bend right knee, grab right foot)
- **Bow Pose**: 30s (lying on stomach, grab ankles, kick legs back to lift chest)
- **Low Prayer Squat (Rocking)**: 45s (deep squat, elbows inside knees)
- **Frog Stretch**: 60s (knees wide, feet out, hips push back)

---

### 7. Hamstring & Calf Focus
Performed once sequentially.
- **Seated Single-Leg Hamstring Stretch (Left straight)**: 60s (right foot to inner thigh, fold forward)
- **Seated Single-Leg Hamstring Stretch (Right straight)**: 60s (left foot to inner thigh, fold forward)
- **Seated Two-Leg Hamstring Stretch**: 60s (both legs straight, feet flexed, fold forward)
- **Ballistic Hamstring Stretch**: 60s (lying on back, 10 alternating straight leg lifts)
- **Standing Split-Leg Hamstring Stretch (Right forward)**: 45s (pyramid pose, drive left hip down)
- **Standing Split-Leg Hamstring Stretch (Left forward)**: 45s (pyramid pose, drive right hip down)

---

### 8. Ankle, Calf & Feet Mobilization
Performed once sequentially.
- **Ankle Point-Flex (Right)**: 15s (8 reps)
- **Ankle Circles Inward/Outward (Right)**: 30s (8 reps each)
- **Ankle Point-Flex (Left)**: 15s (8 reps)
- **Ankle Circles Inward/Outward (Left)**: 30s (8 reps each)
- **Downward Dog (Alternating Calf Stretch)**: 60s (pedaling feet)
- **Upward Dog with Ankle Stretch**: 30s (hip drop, chest up, feet wide and toes flat)

---

### 9. Cool Down
Performed once sequentially.
- **Child's Pose (Center)**: 30s
- **Child's Pose (Right side stretch)**: 30s (hands side-walked right, left hand on top)
- **Child's Pose (Left side stretch)**: 30s (hands side-walked left, right hand on top) [Transcript Cutoff]
